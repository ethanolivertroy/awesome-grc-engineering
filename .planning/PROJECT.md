# Awesome GRC Engineering

## What This Is

A curated, opinionated list of resources for GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) engineering — the discipline that bridges traditional business-focused GRC with technical implementation. For GRC professionals seeking technical depth and security engineers needing to understand the business side of risk and compliance.

## Core Value

Become THE canonical reference the GRC engineering community points to when someone asks "where do I learn about GRC engineering?"

## Context

**The Gap**: Traditional GRC resources focus on policy and business risk. Engineering resources focus on technical implementation. The connection between them — how to actually build compliant systems, automate evidence collection, enable the business to take smart risks — is underserved.

**GRC Engineering Philosophy** (from the manifesto at grc.engineering):
- **Values**: Automation over manual, GRC-as-Code, meaningful outcomes over checkboxes, evidence-driven, continuous assurance, user-centered design, partnership models, open-source preference
- **Principles**: Shift left, practitioner-led, treat GRC as a product, threat-informed, systems thinking

**Dual Audience**:
- GRC professionals: Want to move beyond spreadsheets and manual processes, learn to automate, understand technical implementation
- Security engineers: Need to understand why compliance matters, how to express business metrics, how to enable (not block) the business

**What makes this different**: Not just a link dump. Each resource includes commentary on *why* it matters and *who* it's for (GRC→technical or technical→GRC).
